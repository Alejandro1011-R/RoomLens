01

Positioning

RoomLens exists because listing photos are technically true and practically dishonest. The brand's whole job is to make honesty feel like a feature, not a complaint.

Category

3D computer vision for room rentals. Photos in, a measured walkthrough out.

Who it serves

Renters moving city or country sight-unseen, room owners tired of wasted viewings, and platforms that need trust.

The enemy

The reality gap — the distance between a 14 mm wide-angle photo and the room you actually stand in.

Proof, not adjectives

We never say "spacious". We show 6.9 m², a ceiling height and a dimensioned plan.

Primary lineSo the room you rent is the room you see.
Product lineWalk through any room before you ever leave home.
02

Logo

The mark is a room drawn in one-point perspective: an outer frame — the lens — a back wall set slightly off-centre so the viewer is standing somewhere specific rather than floating, and the floor plane filled in accent, because the measured floor is what we actually hand back. Four edges converge on the vanishing point.

Sizes & clear space

Clear space

Free margin on all sides equal to the height of the inner square — roughly 27 % of the mark. Nothing enters it.

Minimum size

Icon alone: 20 px / 6 mm. Full lockup: 96 px / 24 mm. Below that, drop the wordmark.

App icon

Icon only, centred on Deep Ink with 18 % padding. Never the lockup.

Rules

Do

Use the wordmark with "Lens" in accent on neutral grounds, and fully single-colour on anything busy.

Don't

Rotate, skew, outline or shadow the mark. It's a perspective drawing — tilting it breaks the geometry.

Don't

Recolour the floor plane to anything but Cobalt Blue, or the current single colour. That plane is the one fixed idea.

Don't

Place the lockup on a photo without a solid plate behind it. Contrast is a requirement for us, not a preference.

03

Colour

Two cool signals, told apart by hue rather than by warmth. Cobalt Blue is the instrument and everything actionable; Depth Green is any figure that has actually been measured. They sit 66° apart in hue because the reality-gap readout puts them side by side. The neutrals stay plaster: warm-biased, never pure grey — the warmth now lives entirely in the ground, which is what keeps a cobalt page from reading cold.

The 80/15/5 rule. Roughly 80 % neutral, 15 % ink, 5 % accent. Cobalt is the only loud colour on the page — if two cobalt things compete for attention in one viewport, one of them is wrong. Green never acts as a second accent: it means "verified", and nothing else.
04

Typography

Three faces, three jobs, and one rule above all: weight is restraint. Display type never goes above 600, because size and tracking carry the hierarchy instead of heaviness. Familjen Grotesk is the display face, Instrument Sans the body, IBM Plex Mono every number and label — measurements should look measured.

05

Voice

Plain, specific, slightly dry. We're the side that measures, so confidence comes from the numbers — the sentences don't have to shout.

Measured, not marketed

Prefer a figure to an adjective. "6.9 m² usable" beats "surprisingly spacious" every time.

Name the gap, don't moan

We're against a lens, not against landlords. Never scold the reader or the market.

Say what happens next

A button says exactly what it does, and the message afterwards confirms it in the same words.

Short sentences, real nouns

Scan, room, listing, floor plan — not "immersive spatial solution".

Rewrites

Instead ofRevolutionary AI-powered immersive spatial experiences for the rental ecosystem.
WritePhotos in, a walkable 3D room out. Measured in real metres.
Instead ofBright, spacious double room in a fantastic location!

06

Components

Structure comes from hairlines, not from boxes. Border, fill and shadow each announce "a separate object", so they're spent by role: one filled button per view, everything else quiet. Corner radius stays at 2–3 px — near-square reads as drafting, not as a consumer app.

Actions

Request a demo See the reality gap → One filled button per view. The secondary action is a ruled link, not a second button.

Motion & the 3D viewer

  • The 3D room renders at page load, already visible — never behind a "click to load" placeholder.
  • Idle auto-rotation is slow, about 0.0016 rad per frame, and stops permanently on first user input.
  • Walls are front-face culled with normals pointing inward: a dollhouse from outside, a sealed room from inside. One geometry, both views.
  • Transitions are 180–220 ms with a default ease. No bounce, no scroll-jacking, no parallax.
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables auto-rotation and the pulse indicator. Drag still works.
07

Handoff

What engineering receives, and what "done" means.

FileWhat it is
landing.htmlFull landing page, self-contained. Three.js r128 from cdnjs, two WebGL scenes, procedural team portraits. Rebuild in your stack or ship as-is.
tokens.cssSource of truth for colour, type, spacing and radii, with light and dark blocks plus the base rules. Port to tailwind.config.js if you prefer.
brandbook.htmlThis document. Keep it in the repo — it's the reference when someone asks "what colour should this be".
logo/roomlens-isotipo.svgIcon, full colour.
logo/roomlens-isotipo-mono.svgIcon, single colour via currentColor — inherits from its container.
logo/roomlens-isotipo-inverso.svgIcon for dark grounds.
logo/roomlens-lockup-horizontal.svgIcon plus wordmark. Convert the text to outlines before any print or app-store use.

Definition of done

  • Light and dark both render correctly in all three theme states: OS light, OS dark, and an explicit toggle that beats the OS.
  • The page body never scrolls sideways at 320 px; wide content scrolls inside its own container.
  • Every colour comes from a token. No literal hex values in component CSS.
  • Keyboard: the 3D viewer is focusable and orbits with the arrow keys; every control shows a visible focus ring.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts with a real fallback stack declared — no silent fallback to a system default.
  • The six team members come from one data array; names and roles are editable without touching markup.
  • Display text never exceeds weight 600, and running text never exceeds 50 characters per line.
  • Copy is English throughout; measurements in metres with a decimal point.
Two things still open. Four team names are placeholders in the TEAM array — swap them and the cards update on their own. And the portraits are procedural depth-scan graphics: once real photos exist, drop them into the same 4:3 container and delete drawAvatar().
